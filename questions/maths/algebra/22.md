## The LCM of $x^3 - a^3$ and $(x - a)^2$ is

---

#### Factorizing each expression:
1. $x^3 - a^3 = (x - a)(x^2 + ax + a^2)$
2. $(x - a)^2 = (x - a)^2$

The Least Common Multiple (LCM) contains the highest power of each distinct factor:
$$	ext{LCM} = (x - a)^2(x^2 + ax + a^2)$$
