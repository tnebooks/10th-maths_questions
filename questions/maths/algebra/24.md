## The LCM of $6x^2y$, $9x^2yz$, $12x^2y^2z$ is

---

#### To find the LCM of expressions, find the LCM of the numerical coefficients and the highest power of each variable:

1. Numerical coefficients: $6, 9, 12$
   $$6 = 2 \times 3, \quad 9 = 3^2, \quad 12 = 2^2 \times 3$$
   $$\text{LCM}(6, 9, 12) = 2^2 \times 3^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36$$

2. Variable powers:
   - Highest power of $x$ is $x^2$
   - Highest power of $y$ is $y^2$
   - Highest power of $z$ is $z$

Therefore:
$$\text{LCM} = 36x^2y^2z$$
