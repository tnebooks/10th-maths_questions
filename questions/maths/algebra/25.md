## The solution of $(2x-1)^2 = 9$ is

---

#### Take square roots on both sides of $(2x-1)^2 = 9$. Since a square root can be positive or negative:
$$2x - 1 = \pm 3$$

**Case 1:** $2x - 1 = 3$
$$2x = 4 \implies x = 2$$

**Case 2:** $2x - 1 = -3$
$$2x = -2 \implies x = -1$$

**Verification:**
- $x = 2$: $(2(2)-1)^2 = (3)^2 = 9$ ✓
- $x = -1$: $(2(-1)-1)^2 = (-3)^2 = 9$ ✓

Both values satisfy the equation.

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{x = -1, \ 2}$$
