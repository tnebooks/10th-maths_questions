## The roots of the equation $x^2 - 25 = 0$ are

---

#### The given quadratic equation is $x^2 - 25 = 0$.

Solving directly:
$$x^2 = 25 \implies x = \pm 5$$

The roots are $+5$ and $-5$. Since both are real numbers and $+5 \neq -5$, the roots are **real and unequal**.

Alternatively, using the discriminant $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$:
Here $a = 1$, $b = 0$, and $c = -25$.
$$\Delta = 0^2 - 4(1)(-25) = 100 > 0$$

Since $\Delta > 0$, the roots are **real and unequal**.
