## A system of three linear equations in three variables is inconsistent if their planes

---

#### A system of linear equations is said to be inconsistent if it has no solution at all. In three-dimensional space, each linear equation in three variables represents a plane. If the three planes intersected at a single point, that point would be the unique solution of the system; if they intersected in a common line, every point on that line would be a solution — either way the system would be consistent. Since an inconsistent system has no common point or line shared by all three planes, the only possibility left is that the planes do not intersect at all (they may be parallel, or pairwise intersecting without a common point/line shared by all three).

**Answer:** The planes **do not intersect**.
