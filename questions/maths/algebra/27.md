## For the given matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & 5 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$, the order of the matrix $(A^T)^T$ is

---

#### For any matrix $A$, the transpose of the transpose matrix is the original matrix itself:
$$(A^T)^T = A$$

The given matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & 5 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$ has $2$ rows and $3$ columns.

Therefore, the order of $(A^T)^T$, which equals the order of $A$, is **$2 \times 3$**.
