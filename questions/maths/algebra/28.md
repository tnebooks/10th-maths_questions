## The values of $a$ and $b$ if $4x^4-24x^3+76x^2+ax+b$ is a perfect square are

---

#### Since $4x^4-24x^3+76x^2+ax+b$ has leading term $4x^4=(2x^2)^2$, assume it is the square of a quadratic of the form $(2x^2+px+q)^2$.

**Step 1: Expand $(2x^2+px+q)^2$.**
$$(2x^2+px+q)^2 = 4x^4+4px^3+(p^2+4q)x^2+2pqx+q^2$$

**Step 2: Match coefficients with $4x^4-24x^3+76x^2+ax+b$.**

Coefficient of $x^3$:
$$4p = -24 \implies p = -6$$

Coefficient of $x^2$:
$$p^2+4q = 76 \implies 36+4q = 76 \implies 4q = 40 \implies q = 10$$

Coefficient of $x$ (this is $a$):
$$a = 2pq = 2(-6)(10) = -120$$

Constant term (this is $b$):
$$b = q^2 = 10^2 = 100$$

**Step 3: Verify.**

With $p=-6, q=10$: $(2x^2-6x+10)^2 = 4x^4-24x^3+(36+40)x^2-120x+100 = 4x^4-24x^3+76x^2-120x+100$, which matches $4x^4-24x^3+76x^2+ax+b$ with $a=-120, b=100$. ✓

**Answer:** $a=-120,\ b=100$
