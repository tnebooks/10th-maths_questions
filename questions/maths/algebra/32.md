## Simplify: $\dfrac{1}{x^2-5x+6} + \dfrac{1}{x^2-3x+2} - \dfrac{1}{x^2-8x+15}$

---

#### Factorize each denominator first.

$$x^2-5x+6 = (x-2)(x-3)$$
$$x^2-3x+2 = (x-1)(x-2)$$
$$x^2-8x+15 = (x-3)(x-5)$$

So the expression becomes:
$$\frac{1}{(x-2)(x-3)} + \frac{1}{(x-1)(x-2)} - \frac{1}{(x-3)(x-5)}$$

**Step 1: Find the LCM of the denominators.**

The distinct linear factors appearing are $(x-1), (x-2), (x-3), (x-5)$, so:
$$\text{LCM} = (x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-5)$$

**Step 2: Write each term over the LCM.**

$$\frac{1}{(x-2)(x-3)} = \frac{(x-1)(x-5)}{(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-5)}$$
$$\frac{1}{(x-1)(x-2)} = \frac{(x-3)(x-5)}{(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-5)}$$
$$\frac{1}{(x-3)(x-5)} = \frac{(x-1)(x-2)}{(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-5)}$$

**Step 3: Combine over the common denominator.**
$$\frac{(x-1)(x-5) + (x-3)(x-5) - (x-1)(x-2)}{(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-5)}$$

**Step 4: Expand each product in the numerator.**
$$(x-1)(x-5) = x^2 - 6x + 5$$
$$(x-3)(x-5) = x^2 - 8x + 15$$
$$(x-1)(x-2) = x^2 - 3x + 2$$

Numerator:
$$(x^2-6x+5) + (x^2-8x+15) - (x^2-3x+2)$$
$$= x^2 - 6x + 5 + x^2 - 8x + 15 - x^2 + 3x - 2$$
$$= (1+1-1)x^2 + (-6-8+3)x + (5+15-2)$$
$$= x^2 - 11x + 18$$

**Step 5: Factorize the numerator.**

We need two numbers whose product is $18$ and sum is $-11$: these are $-2$ and $-9$.
$$x^2 - 11x + 18 = (x-2)(x-9)$$

**Step 6: Substitute back and cancel the common factor $(x-2)$.**
$$\frac{(x-2)(x-9)}{(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-5)} = \frac{x-9}{(x-1)(x-3)(x-5)}$$

**Verification (numerical check at $x=0$):**
Original: $\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{15} = \dfrac{5}{30}+\dfrac{15}{30}-\dfrac{2}{30} = \dfrac{18}{30} = 0.6$

Simplified: $\dfrac{0-9}{(-1)(-3)(-5)} = \dfrac{-9}{-15} = 0.6$ ✓ matches.

**Note on the handwritten answer key:** the key's working appears to retain a factor of $(x-3)$ in the final denominator (a leftover from a prior filing of the same expression). Re-deriving independently step by step (as above) shows the numerator factors as $(x-2)(x-9)$, and it is $(x-2)$ — not $(x-3)$ — that cancels with the LCM, leaving $(x-1)(x-3)(x-5)$ in the denominator. This is confirmed by the numerical check above.

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\dfrac{x-9}{(x-1)(x-3)(x-5)}}$$
