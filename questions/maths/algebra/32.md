## If a polynomial is a perfect square, then its factors will occur in _____ number of times.

---

#### A polynomial $P(x)$ is a perfect square if it can be written as $[Q(x)]^2$ for some polynomial $Q(x)$.

When $Q(x)$ is factorized into linear or irreducible factors:
$$Q(x) = f_1^{a_1} f_2^{a_2} \cdots f_k^{a_k}$$
Then:
$$P(x) = [Q(x)]^2 = f_1^{2a_1} f_2^{2a_2} \cdots f_k^{2a_k}$$

The exponent of every prime factor is $2a_i$, which is always an **even** number.
