## Find the sum to $n$ terms of the series $3+33+333+\ldots$

---

#### Each term of the series $3, 33, 333, \ldots$ is $3$ times a repunit-like number made of $1$'s: $3=3(1), 33=3(11), 333=3(111)$, etc. So:
$$S_n = 3(1+11+111+\cdots \text{to } n \text{ terms})$$

**Step 1: Rewrite each term of $(1+11+111+\cdots)$ using powers of $10$.**

Multiply and divide by $9$:
$$1+11+111+\cdots = \frac{1}{9}(9+99+999+\cdots \text{to } n \text{ terms})$$

Each term $9, 99, 999, \ldots$ can be written as $(10-1), (100-1), (1000-1), \ldots$, i.e. $(10^k-1)$ for $k=1,2,\ldots,n$:
$$9+99+999+\cdots = (10^1-1)+(10^2-1)+(10^3-1)+\cdots+(10^n-1)$$
$$= (10^1+10^2+\cdots+10^n) - n$$

**Step 2: Sum the geometric series $10^1+10^2+\cdots+10^n$.**

This is a GP with first term $10$, common ratio $10$, and $n$ terms:
$$10^1+10^2+\cdots+10^n = \frac{10(10^n-1)}{10-1} = \frac{10(10^n-1)}{9}$$

So:
$$9+99+999+\cdots = \frac{10(10^n-1)}{9} - n$$

**Step 3: Combine the factors.**
$$1+11+111+\cdots = \frac{1}{9}\left[\frac{10(10^n-1)}{9} - n\right] = \frac{10(10^n-1)-9n}{81}$$

$$S_n = 3\times\frac{10(10^n-1)-9n}{81} = \frac{10(10^n-1)-9n}{27}$$

**Step 4: Verify.**

For $n=1$: $S_1 = \dfrac{10(10-1)-9(1)}{27} = \dfrac{90-9}{27} = \dfrac{81}{27} = 3$ ✓ (matches the first term $3$).

For $n=2$: $S_2 = \dfrac{10(100-1)-9(2)}{27} = \dfrac{990-18}{27} = \dfrac{972}{27} = 36$ ✓ (matches $3+33=36$).

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{S_n = \dfrac{10(10^n-1)-9n}{27}}$$
