## If $P=\dfrac{x^2-36}{x^2-49}$, $Q=\dfrac{x+6}{x+7}$ then find the value of $\dfrac{P}{Q}$

---

#### Factorize the numerator and denominator of $P$ using the difference of squares identity $a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$.

**Step 1: Factorize $P$.**
$$P = \frac{x^2-36}{x^2-49} = \frac{x^2-6^2}{x^2-7^2} = \frac{(x-6)(x+6)}{(x-7)(x+7)}$$

**Step 2: Write $\dfrac{P}{Q}$ as a division of fractions.**
$$\frac{P}{Q} = \frac{(x-6)(x+6)}{(x-7)(x+7)} \div \frac{x+6}{x+7}$$

**Step 3: Convert division into multiplication by the reciprocal.**
$$\frac{P}{Q} = \frac{(x-6)(x+6)}{(x-7)(x+7)} \times \frac{x+7}{x+6}$$

**Step 4: Cancel the common factors $(x+6)$ and $(x+7)$.**
$$\frac{P}{Q} = \frac{(x-6)\cancel{(x+6)}}{(x-7)\cancel{(x+7)}} \times \frac{\cancel{(x+7)}}{\cancel{(x+6)}} = \frac{x-6}{x-7}$$

**Step 5: Verify by substituting a numeric value, say $x=10$.**

$P = \dfrac{100-36}{100-49} = \dfrac{64}{51}$, $Q = \dfrac{16}{17}$.

$\dfrac{P}{Q} = \dfrac{64}{51}\times\dfrac{17}{16} = \dfrac{64\times17}{51\times16} = \dfrac{1088}{816} = \dfrac{4}{3}$ (dividing numerator and denominator by $272$).

Direct formula: $\dfrac{x-6}{x-7} = \dfrac{10-6}{10-7} = \dfrac{4}{3}$ ✓ matches.

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\frac{P}{Q} = \frac{x-6}{x-7}}$$
