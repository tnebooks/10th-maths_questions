## Solve the following system of linear equations in three variables: $3x-2y+z=2,\ 2x+3y-z=5,\ x+y+z=6$

---

#### Label the equations and eliminate one variable at a time.

Let:
$$3x-2y+z=2 \quad \text{...(1)}$$
$$2x+3y-z=5 \quad \text{...(2)}$$
$$x+y+z=6 \quad \text{...(3)}$$

**Step 1: Eliminate $z$ using (1) and (2).**

Adding (1) and (2) (the $z$ and $-z$ terms cancel):
$$(3x+2x)+(-2y+3y)+(z-z)=2+5$$
$$5x+y=7 \quad \text{...(4)}$$

**Step 2: Eliminate $z$ using (1) and (3).**

From (3): $z=6-x-y$. Substitute into (1):
$$3x-2y+(6-x-y)=2$$
$$2x-3y+6=2$$
$$2x-3y=-4 \quad \text{...(5)}$$

**Step 3: Solve (4) and (5) for $x$ and $y$.**

From (4): $y=7-5x$. Substitute into (5):
$$2x-3(7-5x)=-4$$
$$2x-21+15x=-4$$
$$17x=17$$
$$x=1$$

Then: $y=7-5(1)=2$

**Step 4: Find $z$ using (3).**
$$z=6-x-y=6-1-2=3$$

**Step 5: Verify in equation (2).**
$$2(1)+3(2)-3=2+6-3=5 ✓$$

Verify in equation (1): $3(1)-2(2)+3=3-4+3=2$ ✓

Verify in equation (3): $1+2+3=6$ ✓

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{x=1,\ y=2,\ z=3}$$
