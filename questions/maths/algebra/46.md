## In a place, a flock of $x^2$ swans was present. Due to the gathering of clouds, $10x$ swans went to the lake. One-eighth of the total number of swans flew to the garden. The remaining three pairs played in the water. Find the total number of swans.

---

#### Translate each condition into an equation in $x$ using the total $x^2$, then solve the resulting quadratic. "Three pairs" means $3 \times 2 = 6$ swans.

**Step 1: Set up the equation**

Total swans $= x^2$.
Swans that went to the lake $= 10x$.
Swans that flew to the garden $= \dfrac{x^2}{8}$ (one-eighth of the total).
Swans remaining (playing in water) $= 3$ pairs $= 6$.

The total splits into these three groups:
$$x^2 = 10x + \frac{x^2}{8} + 6$$

**Step 2: Simplify**

Multiply throughout by $8$ to clear the fraction:
$$8x^2 = 80x + x^2 + 48$$
$$7x^2 - 80x - 48 = 0$$

**Step 3: Solve the quadratic**

Using the quadratic formula with $a=7$, $b=-80$, $c=-48$:
$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a} = \frac{80 \pm \sqrt{6400 + 1344}}{14} = \frac{80 \pm \sqrt{7744}}{14} = \frac{80 \pm 88}{14}$$

$$x = \frac{168}{14} = 12 \quad \text{or} \quad x = \frac{-8}{14} = -\frac{4}{7}$$

Since $x$ must be a positive integer (a count of swans), $x = 12$.

**Step 4: Find the total number of swans**
$$\text{Total} = x^2 = 12^2 = 144$$

**Verification:** Total $= 144$. Lake: $10 \times 12 = 120$. Garden: $144/8 = 18$. Remaining: $144 - 120 - 18 = 6 = 3$ pairs. ✓ All conditions match.

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{144}$$
