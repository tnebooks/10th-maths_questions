## Find the square root of $64x^4 - 16x^3 + 17x^2 - 2x + 1$ by division method.

---

#### Use the long division method for polynomials:

1. **First term:** $\sqrt{64x^4} = 8x^2$.
   Quotient: $8x^2$. Remainder: $-16x^3 + 17x^2$.
2. **Second term:** Double quotient gives $16x^2$.
   Divide $\dfrac{-16x^3}{16x^2} = -x$.
   Multiply $(16x^2 - x)(-x) = -16x^3 + x^2$.
   Subtracting gives remainder $16x^2 - 2x + 1$.
3. **Third term:** Double quotient gives $16x^2 - 2x$.
   Divide $\dfrac{16x^2}{16x^2} = 1$.
   Multiply $(16x^2 - 2x + 1)(1) = 16x^2 - 2x + 1$.
   Subtracting leaves remainder $0$.

Hence:
$$\sqrt{64x^4 - 16x^3 + 17x^2 - 2x + 1} = |8x^2 - x + 1|$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{|8x^2 - x + 1|}$$
