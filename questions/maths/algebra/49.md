## If $A = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 2 & 9 \\ 1 & 2 & 8 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 1 & 2 \\ 5 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$, verify that $(AB)^T = B^T A^T$.

---

#### Given:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 2 & 9 \\ 1 & 2 & 8 \end{pmatrix}_{2 \times 3}, \quad B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 1 & 2 \\ 5 & -1 \end{pmatrix}_{3 \times 2}$$

**Step 1: Compute $AB$**
$$AB = \begin{pmatrix} 5(1) + 2(1) + 9(5) & 5(7) + 2(2) + 9(-1) \\ 1(1) + 2(1) + 8(5) & 1(7) + 2(2) + 8(-1) \end{pmatrix}$$
$$AB = \begin{pmatrix} 5 + 2 + 45 & 35 + 4 - 9 \\ 1 + 2 + 40 & 7 + 4 - 8 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 52 & 30 \\ 43 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$$

**Step 2: Compute $\text{LHS} = (AB)^T$**
$$(AB)^T = \begin{pmatrix} 52 & 43 \\ 30 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \quad \text{--- (1)}$$

**Step 3: Compute $B^T$ and $A^T$**
$$B^T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 5 \\ 7 & 2 & -1 \end{pmatrix}_{2 \times 3}, \quad A^T = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \\ 9 & 8 \end{pmatrix}_{3 \times 2}$$

**Step 4: Compute $\text{RHS} = B^T A^T$**
$$B^T A^T = \begin{pmatrix} 1(5) + 1(2) + 5(9) & 1(1) + 1(2) + 5(8) \\ 7(5) + 2(2) + (-1)(9) & 7(1) + 2(2) + (-1)(8) \end{pmatrix}$$
$$B^T A^T = \begin{pmatrix} 5 + 2 + 45 & 1 + 2 + 40 \\ 35 + 4 - 9 & 7 + 4 - 8 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 52 & 43 \\ 30 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \quad \text{--- (2)}$$

**Step 5: Compare (1) and (2)**
From (1) and (2):
$$(AB)^T = B^T A^T$$

Hence, **$(AB)^T = B^T A^T$ is verified.**
