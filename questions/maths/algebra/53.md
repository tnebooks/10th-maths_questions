## The base of a triangle is $4\text{ cm}$ more than its altitude. If the area of the triangle is $48\text{ cm}^2$, find its base and altitude.

---

#### Let the altitude of the triangle be $h\text{ cm}$.

According to the given condition:
$$\text{Base } b = (h + 4)\text{ cm}$$

The formula for the area of a triangle is:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{altitude} = 48\text{ cm}^2$$

$$\frac{1}{2} \times (h + 4) \times h = 48$$
$$h(h + 4) = 96$$
$$h^2 + 4h - 96 = 0$$

Factorizing the quadratic equation:
$$(h + 12)(h - 8) = 0$$

This gives:
$$h = 8 \quad \text{or} \quad h = -12$$

Since length cannot be negative, $h = 8\text{ cm}$.

Now finding the base:
$$b = h + 4 = 8 + 4 = 12\text{ cm}$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\text{Base} = 12\text{ cm}, \quad \text{Altitude} = 8\text{ cm}}$$
