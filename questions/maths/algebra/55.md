## The average age of Vani, her father and her grandfather is $53$. One-half of her grandfather's age plus one-third of her father's age plus one-fourth of Vani's age is $65$. Four years ago, if grandfather was four times as old as Vani, then find their present ages.

---

#### Let the present ages of Vani, her father, and her grandfather be $x$, $y$, and $z$ years respectively.

**Step 1: Form equations from given conditions.**

1. The average age of the three is $53$:
$$\frac{x + y + z}{3} = 53 \implies x + y + z = 159 \quad \text{--- (1)}$$

2. One-fourth of Vani's age plus one-third of her father's age plus one-half of her grandfather's age is $65$:
$$\frac{x}{4} + \frac{y}{3} + \frac{z}{2} = 65$$
Multiply the entire equation by $12$ (LCM of $4, 3, 2$):
$$3x + 4y + 6z = 780 \quad \text{--- (2)}$$

3. Four years ago, grandfather's age was four times Vani's age:
$$z - 4 = 4(x - 4)$$
$$z - 4 = 4x - 16 \implies 4x - z = 12 \implies z = 4x - 12 \quad \text{--- (3)}$$

**Step 2: Solve the linear system.**

Substitute (3) into (1):
$$x + y + (4x - 12) = 159$$
$$5x + y = 171 \implies y = 171 - 5x \quad \text{--- (4)}$$

Substitute (3) and (4) into (2):
$$3x + 4(171 - 5x) + 6(4x - 12) = 780$$
$$3x + 684 - 20x + 24x - 72 = 780$$
$$7x + 612 = 780$$
$$7x = 168 \implies x = 24$$

Now substitute $x = 24$ into (4) and (3):
$$y = 171 - 5(24) = 171 - 120 = 51$$
$$z = 4(24) - 12 = 96 - 12 = 84$$

**Verification:**
- Average: $\frac{24 + 51 + 84}{3} = \frac{159}{3} = 53$ ✓
- $\frac{24}{4} + \frac{51}{3} + \frac{84}{2} = 6 + 17 + 42 = 65$ ✓
- Four years ago: $84 - 4 = 80 = 4(24 - 4) = 4(20)$ ✓

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{Vani = 24\text{ years}, \quad Father = 51\text{ years}, \quad Grandfather = 84\text{ years}}$$
