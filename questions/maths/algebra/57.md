## A motor boat whose speed is $18\text{ km/hr}$ in still water takes $1\text{ hour}$ more to go $24\text{ km}$ upstream than to return downstream to the same spot. Find the speed of the stream.

---

#### Let the speed of the stream be $s\text{ km/hr}$.

- Speed of the boat in still water = $18\text{ km/hr}$
- Speed of the boat upstream (against the stream) = $(18 - s)\text{ km/hr}$
- Speed of the boat downstream (with the stream) = $(18 + s)\text{ km/hr}$
- Distance = $24\text{ km}$

**Step 1: Write expressions for time taken.**
$$\text{Time taken upstream}, \ t_1 = \frac{24}{18 - s}$$
$$\text{Time taken downstream}, \ t_2 = \frac{24}{18 + s}$$

**Step 2: Form and solve the quadratic equation.**
Given that upstream journey takes $1\text{ hour}$ longer than downstream journey:
$$t_1 - t_2 = 1$$
$$\frac{24}{18 - s} - \frac{24}{18 + s} = 1$$
$$24\left[\frac{(18 + s) - (18 - s)}{(18 - s)(18 + s)}\right] = 1$$
$$24\left[\frac{2s}{324 - s^2}\right] = 1$$
$$48s = 324 - s^2$$
$$s^2 + 48s - 324 = 0$$

Factorize the quadratic equation:
Find two numbers whose product is $-324$ and sum is $48$: $54$ and $-6$.
$$(s + 54)(s - 6) = 0$$
$$s + 54 = 0 \implies s = -54$$
$$s - 6 = 0 \implies s = 6$$

Since the speed of a stream cannot be negative, $s = -54$ is rejected.
Thus, $s = 6\text{ km/hr}$.

**Verification:**
- Upstream speed = $18 - 6 = 12\text{ km/hr} \implies t_1 = \frac{24}{12} = 2\text{ hours}$
- Downstream speed = $18 + 6 = 24\text{ km/hr} \implies t_2 = \frac{24}{24} = 1\text{ hour}$
- $t_1 - t_2 = 2 - 1 = 1\text{ hour}$ ✓

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{6\text{ km/hr}}$$
