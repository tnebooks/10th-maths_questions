## The area of the quadrilateral formed by the points $(1, 1)$, $(0, 1)$, $(0, 0)$ and $(1, 0)$ is

---

#### These four points, taken in order, are $A(1,1)$, $B(0,1)$, $C(0,0)$, $D(1,0)$ — the four corners of a unit square.

Area of a quadrilateral with vertices taken in order:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| x_1(y_2 - y_4) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_4 - y_2) + x_4(y_1 - y_3) \right|$$

Substituting $(x_1,y_1)=(1,1)$, $(x_2,y_2)=(0,1)$, $(x_3,y_3)=(0,0)$, $(x_4,y_4)=(1,0)$:
$$x_1(y_2-y_4) = 1(1-0) = 1$$
$$x_2(y_3-y_1) = 0(0-1) = 0$$
$$x_3(y_4-y_2) = 0(0-1) = 0$$
$$x_4(y_1-y_3) = 1(1-0) = 1$$

Adding:
$$1 + 0 + 0 + 1 = 2$$

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}|2| = 1$$

This matches the geometric fact that $(0,0)$, $(1,0)$, $(1,1)$, $(0,1)$ form a square of side $1$, so area $= 1 \times 1 = 1$ sq. unit. $\checkmark$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\text{Area} = 1 \text{ sq. unit}}$$
