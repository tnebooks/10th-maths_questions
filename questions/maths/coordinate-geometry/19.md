## The slope of the straight line which is perpendicular to the line joining the points $(0, 0)$ and $(-8, 8)$ is :

---

#### Slope formula for a line through two points $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$:
$$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$$
Slope of the line joining $(0, 0)$ and $(-8, 8)$:
$$m_1 = \frac{8 - 0}{-8 - 0} = \frac{8}{-8} = -1$$
For two lines to be perpendicular, the product of their slopes must be $-1$:
$$m_1 \times m_2 = -1 \implies (-1) \times m_2 = -1 \implies m_2 = 1$$

**Answer:** The required slope is $\mathbf{1}$.
