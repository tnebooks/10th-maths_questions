## If $(5, 7)$, $(3, p)$ and $(6, 6)$ are collinear, then the value of $p$ is

---

#### Three points are collinear if the slope between any two pairs of them is equal.

Slope of the segment joining $(5, 7)$ and $(3, p)$:
$$m_1 = \frac{p - 7}{3 - 5} = \frac{p - 7}{-2}$$

Slope of the segment joining $(3, p)$ and $(6, 6)$:
$$m_2 = \frac{6 - p}{6 - 3} = \frac{6 - p}{3}$$

Since the points are collinear, $m_1 = m_2$:
$$\frac{p - 7}{-2} = \frac{6 - p}{3}$$

Cross-multiplying:
$$3(p - 7) = -2(6 - p)$$
$$3p - 21 = -12 + 2p$$
$$3p - 2p = -12 + 21$$
$$p = 9$$

**Verification:** With $p = 9$: slope of $(5,7)$-$(3,9)$ is $\frac{9-7}{3-5} = \frac{2}{-2} = -1$; slope of $(3,9)$-$(6,6)$ is $\frac{6-9}{6-3} = \frac{-3}{3} = -1$. Equal, so collinear. $\checkmark$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{p = 9}$$
