## If $A(6, 1)$, $B(8, 2)$, $C(9, 4)$ and $D(p, 3)$ are the vertices of a parallelogram taken in order, then the value of $p$ is

---

#### In a parallelogram, the diagonals bisect each other. Therefore, the midpoint of diagonal $AC$ is equal to the midpoint of diagonal $BD$.

**Midpoint formula:** $\left(\dfrac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \dfrac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$

**Midpoint of $AC$:**
$$\left(\frac{6 + 9}{2}, \frac{1 + 4}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{15}{2}, \frac{5}{2}\right)$$

**Midpoint of $BD$:**
$$\left(\frac{8 + p}{2}, \frac{2 + 3}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{8 + p}{2}, \frac{5}{2}\right)$$

Equating the $x$-coordinates:
$$\frac{8 + p}{2} = \frac{15}{2}$$
$$8 + p = 15 \implies p = 15 - 8 = 7$$

**Answer:** $\mathbf{p = 7}$
