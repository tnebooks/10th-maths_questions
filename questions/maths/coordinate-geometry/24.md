## The equation of the straight line perpendicular to $7x - 3y + 4 = 0$ and passing through the origin is

---

#### First find the slope of the given line $7x - 3y + 4 = 0$.

Writing it in slope form $y = mx + c$:
$$3y = 7x + 4 \implies y = \frac{7}{3}x + \frac{4}{3}$$

So the slope of the given line is $m_1 = \dfrac{7}{3}$.

For a line perpendicular to it, $m_1 \times m_2 = -1$:
$$\frac{7}{3} \times m_2 = -1 \implies m_2 = -\frac{3}{7}$$

The required line passes through the origin $(0, 0)$ with slope $-\dfrac{3}{7}$:
$$y - 0 = -\frac{3}{7}(x - 0)$$
$$7y = -3x$$
$$3x + 7y = 0$$

**Verification:** Slope of $3x + 7y = 0$ is $-\dfrac{3}{7}$. Product with $\dfrac{7}{3}$: $\dfrac{7}{3} \times \left(-\dfrac{3}{7}\right) = -1$. $\checkmark$ Also $(0,0)$ satisfies $3x+7y=0$. $\checkmark$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{3x + 7y = 0}$$
