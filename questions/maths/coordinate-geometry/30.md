## When proving that a quadrilateral is a parallelogram by using slopes you must find

---

#### Recall what makes a quadrilateral a parallelogram.

A quadrilateral is a parallelogram exactly when **both pairs of opposite sides are parallel** to each other.

Since two lines are parallel if and only if they have the **same slope**, proving a quadrilateral is a parallelogram using slopes requires:

1. Finding the slope of one pair of opposite sides and checking they are equal.
2. Finding the slope of the other pair of opposite sides and checking they are equal.

Finding the slope of only one side, or only one pair of sides, is not enough — both pairs of opposite sides must be checked for equal slopes to conclude it is a parallelogram.

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\text{The slopes of two pair of opposite sides}}$$
