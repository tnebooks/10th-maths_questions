## The condition that the lines $a_1x+b_1y+c_1=0$ and $a_2x+b_2y+c_2=0$ are perpendicular if

---

#### Find the slope of each line in general form.

For a line $ax + by + c = 0$, writing it as $y = -\dfrac{a}{b}x - \dfrac{c}{b}$, the slope is:
$$m = -\frac{a}{b}$$

So:
$$m_1 = -\frac{a_1}{b_1}, \qquad m_2 = -\frac{a_2}{b_2}$$

#### Apply the perpendicularity condition $m_1 \times m_2 = -1$.

$$\left(-\frac{a_1}{b_1}\right) \times \left(-\frac{a_2}{b_2}\right) = -1$$
$$\frac{a_1 a_2}{b_1 b_2} = -1$$
$$a_1 a_2 = -b_1 b_2$$
$$a_1 a_2 + b_1 b_2 = 0$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{a_1 a_2 + b_1 b_2 = 0}$$
