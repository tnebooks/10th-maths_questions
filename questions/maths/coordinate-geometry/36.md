## The slope of the line parallel to the line joining the points $(0,0)$ and $(-8,8)$ is

---

#### Step 1: Find the slope of the line joining $(0,0)$ and $(-8,8)$.

$$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{8 - 0}{-8 - 0} = \frac{8}{-8} = -1$$

#### Step 2: Apply the parallel-line rule.

Parallel lines have **equal** slopes (not negative reciprocals, which apply to perpendicular lines). So the slope of any line parallel to this one is also $-1$.

**Verification:** Since parallel lines never meet, their inclination angles are equal, hence $\tan\theta$ (the slope) is identical: $-1$. $\checkmark$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{-1}$$
