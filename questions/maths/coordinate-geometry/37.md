## The $y$-intercept of the line $y = 12x$ is

---

#### Step 1: Recall that the $y$-intercept is the value of $y$ when $x = 0$.

$$y = 12x$$

#### Step 2: Substitute $x = 0$.

$$y = 12(0) = 0$$

**Verification:** The line $y=12x$ passes through the origin $(0,0)$ (it has no constant term), so it must cross the $y$-axis at $y=0$. $\checkmark$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{0}$$
