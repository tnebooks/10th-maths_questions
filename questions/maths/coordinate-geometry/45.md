## A quadrilateral swimming pool is surrounded by a concrete patio. The vertices of the outer boundary are $A(-4, -8)$, $B(8, -4)$, $C(6, 10)$, and $D(-10, 6)$, and the vertices of the swimming pool are $E(-3, -5)$, $F(6, -2)$, $G(3, 7)$, and $H(-6, 4)$. Find the area of the concrete patio.

---

#### The area of the concrete patio is obtained by subtracting the area of the inner quadrilateral swimming pool $EFGH$ from the area of the outer quadrilateral $ABCD$:
$$\text{Area of patio} = \text{Area of quadrilateral } ABCD - \text{Area of quadrilateral } EFGH$$

**Step 1: Area of outer quadrilateral $ABCD$**

Vertices taken in counterclockwise order: $A(-4, -8), B(8, -4), C(6, 10), D(-10, 6)$.
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| (x_1 y_2 + x_2 y_3 + x_3 y_4 + x_4 y_1) - (x_2 y_1 + x_3 y_2 + x_4 y_3 + x_1 y_4) \right|$$

$$\Sigma_1 = (-4)(-4) + 8(10) + 6(6) + (-10)(-8) = 16 + 80 + 36 + 80 = 212$$
$$\Sigma_2 = (-8)(8) + (-4)(6) + 10(-10) + 6(-4) = -64 - 24 - 100 - 24 = -212$$

$$\text{Area}(ABCD) = \frac{1}{2} |212 - (-212)| = \frac{1}{2} (424) = 212\text{ sq. units}$$

**Step 2: Area of inner quadrilateral swimming pool $EFGH$**

Vertices taken in counterclockwise order: $E(-3, -5), F(6, -2), G(3, 7), H(-6, 4)$.
$$\Sigma_1 = (-3)(-2) + 6(7) + 3(4) + (-6)(-5) = 6 + 42 + 12 + 30 = 90$$
$$\Sigma_2 = (-5)(6) + (-2)(3) + 7(-6) + 4(-3) = -30 - 6 - 42 - 12 = -90$$

$$\text{Area}(EFGH) = \frac{1}{2} |90 - (-90)| = \frac{1}{2} (180) = 90\text{ sq. units}$$

**Step 3: Area of the patio**
$$\text{Area of patio} = \text{Area}(ABCD) - \text{Area}(EFGH) = 212 - 90 = 122\text{ sq. units}$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{122\text{ sq. units}}$$
