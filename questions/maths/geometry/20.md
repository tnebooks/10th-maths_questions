## The perimeters of two similar triangles $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle PQR$ are $36\text{ cm}$ and $24\text{ cm}$ respectively. If $PQ = 10\text{ cm}$, then the length of $AB$ is

---

#### For two similar triangles, the ratio of their perimeters equals the ratio of any pair of corresponding sides.
Since $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR$, side $AB$ corresponds to side $PQ$. So:
$$\frac{\text{Perimeter}(\triangle ABC)}{\text{Perimeter}(\triangle PQR)} = \frac{AB}{PQ}$$
Substituting the given values:
$$\frac{36}{24} = \frac{AB}{10}$$
Cross-multiplying:
$$AB = \frac{36 \times 10}{24} = \frac{360}{24} = 15\text{ cm}$$

**Answer:** $\mathbf{15\text{ cm}}$
