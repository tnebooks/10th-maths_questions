## State and prove Thales' Theorem (Basic Proportionality Theorem).

---

#### **Statement:**
A straight line drawn parallel to a side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides divides the sides in the same ratio.

#### **Proof:**

**Given:** In $\triangle ABC$, $D$ is a point on $AB$ and $E$ is a point on $AC$ such that $DE \parallel BC$.

**To prove:** $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$

**Construction:** Draw a line $DE \parallel BC$.

| No. | Statement | Reason |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| 1 | $\angle 1 = \angle 1$ (i.e. $\angle ABC = \angle ADE$) | Corresponding angles, since $DE \parallel BC$ |
| 2 | $\angle 2 = \angle 2$ (i.e. $\angle ACB = \angle AED$) | Corresponding angles, since $DE \parallel BC$ |
| 3 | $\angle 3 = \angle 3$ (i.e. $\angle DAE = \angle BAC$) | Both triangles have a common angle |
| 4 | $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle ADE$ | By AAA similarity |
| 5 | $\dfrac{AB}{AD} = \dfrac{AC}{AE}$ | Corresponding sides are proportional |
| 6 | $\dfrac{AD + DB}{AD} = \dfrac{AE + EC}{AE}$ | Splitting $AB = AD + DB$ and $AC = AE + EC$ |
| 7 | $1 + \dfrac{DB}{AD} = 1 + \dfrac{EC}{AE}$ | On simplifying |
| 8 | $\dfrac{DB}{AD} = \dfrac{EC}{AE}$ | Cancelling $1$ on both sides |
| 9 | $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$ | Taking reciprocals |

Hence, the theorem is proved.
