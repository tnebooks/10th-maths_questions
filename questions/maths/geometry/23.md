## State and prove Pythagoras Theorem.

---

#### **Statement:**
In a right-angled triangle, the square on the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares on the other two sides.

#### **Proof:**

**Given:** In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle A = 90^\circ$.

**To prove:** $AB^2 + AC^2 = BC^2$

**Construction:** Draw $AD \perp BC$.

| No. | Statement | Reason |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| 1 | $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DBA$ | AA similarity (both share $\angle B$, $\angle BAC = \angle BDA = 90^\circ$) |
| | $\dfrac{AB}{BD} = \dfrac{BC}{AB} \implies AB^2 = BC \times BD$ | Corresponding sides are proportional $\quad \text{--- (1)}$ |
| 2 | $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DAC$ | AA similarity (both share $\angle C$, $\angle BAC = \angle CDA = 90^\circ$) |
| | $\dfrac{BC}{AC} = \dfrac{AC}{DC} \implies AC^2 = BC \times DC$ | Corresponding sides are proportional $\quad \text{--- (2)}$ |
| 3 | $AB^2 + AC^2 = BC \times BD + BC \times DC$ | Adding (1) and (2) |
| | $= BC(BD + DC)$ | Factoring out $BC$ |
| | $= BC \times BC = BC^2$ | Since $BD + DC = BC$ |
| 4 | $\mathbf{AB^2 + AC^2 = BC^2}$ | Hence proved |

Hence, the Pythagoras Theorem is proved.
