## If the radii of the circular ends of a frustum which is $45\text{ cm}$ high are $28\text{ cm}$ and $7\text{ cm}$, find the volume of the frustum.

---

#### Given:
- Height of frustum, $h = 45\text{ cm}$
- Top radius, $R = 28\text{ cm}$
- Bottom radius, $r = 7\text{ cm}$

The volume of a frustum of a cone is:
$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi h (R^2 + Rr + r^2)$$

**Step 1: Compute $R^2 + Rr + r^2$**
$$R^2 = 28^2 = 784$$
$$Rr = 28 \times 7 = 196$$
$$r^2 = 7^2 = 49$$
$$R^2 + Rr + r^2 = 784 + 196 + 49 = 1029$$

**Step 2: Calculate the volume**
$$V = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 45 \times 1029$$
$$V = 15 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 1029$$
Since $1029 \div 7 = 147$:
$$V = 15 \times 22 \times 147 = 330 \times 147 = 48510\text{ cm}^3$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{48510\text{ cm}^3}$$
