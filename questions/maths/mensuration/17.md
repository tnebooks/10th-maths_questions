## The radius of a sphere having volume $36\pi\text{ cm}^3$ is

---

#### The volume of a sphere of radius $r$ is given by:
$$V = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$$

Given that the volume is $36\pi\text{ cm}^3$:
$$\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = 36\pi$$

Cancelling $\pi$ from both sides:
$$\dfrac{4}{3}r^3 = 36$$
$$r^3 = 36 \times \dfrac{3}{4} = 9 \times 3 = 27$$
$$r = \sqrt[3]{27} = 3\text{ cm}$$

Therefore, the radius of the sphere is $3\text{ cm}$.
