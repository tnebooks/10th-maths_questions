## The sum of the external and internal radii of a hollow cylinder is $14\text{ cm}$ and its thickness is $4\text{ cm}$. If the height of the cylinder is $20\text{ cm}$, the volume of the material is

---

#### Let $R$ and $r$ be the external and internal radii, and $h$ be the height of the hollow cylinder.

Given:
- Sum of radii: $R + r = 14\text{ cm}$
- Thickness: $R - r = 4\text{ cm}$
- Height: $h = 20\text{ cm}$

The volume of a hollow cylinder is:
$$V = \pi(R^2 - r^2)h = \pi(R + r)(R - r)h$$

Substituting the given values:
$$V = \pi \times 14 \times 4 \times 20 = \pi \times 56 \times 20 = 1120\pi\text{ cm}^3$$
