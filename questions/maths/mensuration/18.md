## The curved surface area of a sphere is equal to

---

#### A sphere consists of a single continuous curved surface.

The curved surface area (CSA) of a sphere of radius $r$ is:
$$\text{CSA} = 4\pi r^2$$

The total surface area (TSA) of a sphere of radius $r$ is also:
$$\text{TSA} = 4\pi r^2$$

For comparison:
- Curved surface area of a hemisphere $= 2\pi r^2$
- Total surface area of a hemisphere $= 3\pi r^2$

Therefore, the curved surface area of a sphere is equal to the **total surface area of a sphere**.
