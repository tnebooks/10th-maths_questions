## A container open at the top is in the form of a frustum of a cone of height $16\text{ cm}$ with radii of its lower and upper ends as $8\text{ cm}$ and $20\text{ cm}$ respectively. Find the cost of milk which can completely fill a container at the rate of ₹40 per litre.

---

#### Given:
- Height of container, $h = 16\text{ cm}$
- Lower radius, $r = 8\text{ cm}$
- Upper radius, $R = 20\text{ cm}$
- Cost of milk = ₹40 per litre

**Step 1: Find volume of the frustum**
$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi h (R^2 + Rr + r^2)$$
$$R^2 + Rr + r^2 = 20^2 + 20(8) + 8^2 = 400 + 160 + 64 = 624$$

$$V = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 16 \times 624$$
$$V = \frac{22}{7} \times 16 \times 208 = \frac{73216}{7} = 10459.43\text{ cm}^3$$

**Step 2: Convert volume into litres**
$$1000\text{ cm}^3 = 1\text{ litre}$$
$$\text{Volume in litres} = \frac{10459.43}{1000} = 10.459\text{ litres}$$

**Step 3: Calculate the total cost**
$$\text{Cost} = 10.459 \times 40 = ₹418.36$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{₹418.36}$$
