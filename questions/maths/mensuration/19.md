## How many circular coins $1.5\text{ cm}$ in diameter and of thickness $2\text{ mm}$ must be melted to form a right circular cylinder of height $10\text{ cm}$ and diameter $4.5\text{ cm}$?

---

#### **1. Dimensions of a coin (small cylinder):**
- Diameter $= 1.5\text{ cm} \implies \text{radius } r = \dfrac{1.5}{2} = 0.75\text{ cm} = \dfrac{3}{4}\text{ cm}$
- Thickness $\text{(height) } h = 2\text{ mm} = 0.2\text{ cm} = \dfrac{1}{5}\text{ cm}$

Volume of one coin:
$$V_{\text{coin}} = \pi r^2 h = \pi \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^2 \left(\frac{1}{5}\right) = \pi \times \frac{9}{16} \times \frac{1}{5} = \frac{9\pi}{80}\text{ cm}^3$$

**2. Dimensions of the required cylinder:**
- Diameter $= 4.5\text{ cm} \implies \text{radius } R = \dfrac{4.5}{2} = \dfrac{9}{4}\text{ cm}$
- Height $H = 10\text{ cm}$

Volume of the cylinder:
$$V_{\text{cyl}} = \pi R^2 H = \pi \left(\frac{9}{4}\right)^2 \times 10 = \pi \times \frac{81}{16} \times 10 = \frac{405\pi}{8}\text{ cm}^3$$

**3. Number of coins required ($n$):**
$$n = \frac{V_{\text{cyl}}}{V_{\text{coin}}} = \frac{\frac{405\pi}{8}}{\frac{9\pi}{80}} = \frac{405}{8} \times \frac{80}{9} = \left(\frac{405}{9}\right) \times \left(\frac{80}{8}\right) = 45 \times 10 = 450$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{450\text{ coins}}$$
