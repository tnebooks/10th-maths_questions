## A funnel consists of a frustum of a cone attached to a cylindrical portion $12\text{ cm}$ long attached at the bottom. If the total height be $20\text{ cm}$, diameter of the cylindrical portion be $12\text{ cm}$ and the diameter of the top of the funnel be $24\text{ cm}$, find the outer surface area of the funnel.

---

#### Let:
- Top radius of frustum $R = \dfrac{24}{2} = 12\text{ cm}$
- Bottom radius of frustum (same as cylinder radius) $r = \dfrac{12}{2} = 6\text{ cm}$
- Height of cylinder $h_2 = 12\text{ cm}$
- Total height of funnel $= 20\text{ cm} \implies \text{height of frustum } h_1 = 20 - 12 = 8\text{ cm}$

**1. Slant height of the frustum ($l$):**
$$l = \sqrt{(R - r)^2 + h_1^2} = \sqrt{(12 - 6)^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10\text{ cm}$$

**2. Outer surface area of the funnel:**
$$\text{Outer Surface Area} = \text{CSA of cylinder} + \text{CSA of frustum}$$
$$= 2\pi r h_2 + \pi (R + r)l$$
$$= \pi [2r h_2 + (R + r)l]$$
$$= \pi [2(6)(12) + (12 + 6)(10)]$$
$$= \pi [144 + 180] = 324\pi$$
$$= \frac{22}{7} \times 324 \approx 1018.28\text{ cm}^2$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{1018.28\text{ cm}^2}$$
