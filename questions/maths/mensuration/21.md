## The radius of a sector of a circle is $21\text{ cm}$ and its central angle is $216^\circ$. It is bent to form a cone by joining its bounding radii. Find the volume of the cone.

---

#### Let radius of sector $R = 21\text{ cm}$ and central angle $\theta = 216^\circ$.

When the sector is folded to form a cone by joining its bounding radii:
1. The slant height of the cone $l$ equals the radius of the sector:
$$l = R = 21\text{ cm}$$

2. The circumference of the circular base of the cone equals the arc length of the sector:
$$2\pi r = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi R$$
$$r = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times R = \frac{216^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 21 = \frac{3}{5} \times 21 = \frac{63}{5} = 12.6\text{ cm}$$

3. Find the vertical height $h$ of the cone:
$$h = \sqrt{l^2 - r^2} = \sqrt{21^2 - (12.6)^2} = \sqrt{441 - 158.76} = \sqrt{282.24} = 16.8\text{ cm}$$

4. Calculate the volume of the cone:
$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$$
$$V = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times (12.6)^2 \times 16.8$$
$$V = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 158.76 \times 16.8$$
$$V = \frac{22}{7} \times 52.92 \times 16.8 = 22 \times 7.56 \times 16.8 = 2794.18\text{ cm}^3$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{2794.18\text{ cm}^3}$$
