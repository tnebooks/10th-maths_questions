## A shuttle cock used for playing badminton has the shape of the combination of the frustum of a cone mounted on a hemisphere. The diameters of the frustum are $5\text{ cm}$ and $2\text{ cm}$, and the total height of the shuttle cock is $7\text{ cm}$. Find the external surface area.

---

#### The shuttle cock is composed of two solids:
1. A frustum of a cone at the top.
2. A hemisphere at the bottom.

**Step 1: Determine the dimensions of the parts.**
- Diameter of lower base of frustum = Diameter of hemisphere = $2\text{ cm} \implies r = 1\text{ cm}$.
- Radius of hemisphere = $r = 1\text{ cm}$.
- Height of hemisphere = $r = 1\text{ cm}$.
- Diameter of upper base of frustum = $5\text{ cm} \implies R = 2.5\text{ cm}$.
- Total height of shuttle cock = $7\text{ cm}$.
- Height of frustum, $h = 7 - 1 = 6\text{ cm}$.

**Step 2: Find the slant height $l$ of the frustum.**
$$l = \sqrt{h^2 + (R - r)^2} = \sqrt{6^2 + (2.5 - 1)^2} = \sqrt{36 + 1.5^2} = \sqrt{36 + 2.25} = \sqrt{38.25} \approx 6.18\text{ cm}$$

**Step 3: Calculate external surface area.**
$$\text{External Surface Area} = \text{CSA of frustum} + \text{CSA of hemisphere}$$
$$\text{CSA of frustum} = \pi (R + r) l = \frac{22}{7} (2.5 + 1)(6.18) = \frac{22}{7} (3.5)(6.18) = 11 \times 6.18 = 67.98\text{ cm}^2$$
$$\text{CSA of hemisphere} = 2\pi r^2 = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 1^2 = \frac{44}{7} \approx 6.29\text{ cm}^2$$

$$\text{Total external surface area} = 67.98 + 6.29 = 74.27\text{ cm}^2$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{74.27\text{ cm}^2}$$
