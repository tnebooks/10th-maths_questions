#### By Euclid's Division Lemma, when a positive integer $a$ is divided by a positive integer $b$, there exist unique integers $q$ (quotient) and $r$ (remainder) such that $a = bq + r$. The remainder $r$ must be non-negative (it cannot be negative since it represents what is left over after dividing as many times as possible) and it must be strictly less than the divisor $b$ (otherwise one more division would be possible). Hence, $0 \leq r < b$.
