## If 6 times the 6th term of an Arithmetic Progression is equal to 7 times its 7th term, then the 13th term of the A.P. is

---

#### Let the first term be $a$ and the common difference be $d$. Using $t_n = a + (n-1)d$:
$$t_6 = a + 5d, \qquad t_7 = a + 6d$$
Given: $6 \, t_6 = 7 \, t_7$
$$6(a + 5d) = 7(a + 6d)$$
$$6a + 30d = 7a + 42d$$
$$0 = a + 12d$$
$$a + 12d = 0$$
But $t_{13} = a + 12d$ (since $t_{13} = a + (13-1)d = a+12d$). Therefore $t_{13} = 0$.
