## $7^{4k} \equiv$ ___ (mod 100)

---

#### Find the pattern of powers of $7$ modulo $100$:
$$7^1 = 7 \pmod{100}$$
$$7^2 = 49 \pmod{100}$$
$$7^3 = 343 \equiv 43 \pmod{100}$$
$$7^4 = 7^3 \times 7 = 43 \times 7 = 301 \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$$

Since $7^4 \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$, raising both sides to the power $k$:
$$7^{4k} = (7^4)^k \equiv 1^k \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$$

**Answer:** $7^{4k} \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$
