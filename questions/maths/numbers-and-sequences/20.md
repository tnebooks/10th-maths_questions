## The smallest number divisible by all numbers from $1$ to $10$ (both inclusive) is

---

#### The smallest number divisible by every number from $1$ to $10$ is the LCM of $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 10$.

Prime factorize each number:
$$2 = 2, \quad 3 = 3, \quad 4 = 2^2, \quad 5 = 5, \quad 6 = 2\times 3, \quad 7 = 7, \quad 8 = 2^3, \quad 9 = 3^2, \quad 10 = 2 \times 5$$

The LCM takes the highest power of each prime appearing:
$$\text{LCM} = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5 \times 7$$
$$= 8 \times 9 \times 5 \times 7 = 72 \times 35 = 2520$$

**Answer:** $2520$
