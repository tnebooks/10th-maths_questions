## Find the sum of all natural numbers between $300$ and $600$ which are divisible by $7$.

---

#### We need the natural numbers strictly between $300$ and $600$ that are divisible by $7$. These numbers form an Arithmetic Progression.

**Step 1: Find the first term**

The smallest multiple of $7$ greater than $300$:
$$300 \div 7 = 42.86\ldots$$
So $7 \times 43 = 301$, which is greater than $300$. Hence the first term is $a = 301$.

**Step 2: Find the last term**

The largest multiple of $7$ less than $600$:
$$600 \div 7 = 85.71\ldots$$
So $7 \times 85 = 595$, which is less than $600$. Hence the last term is $l = 595$.

**Step 3: Find the number of terms**

The sequence is an A.P. with first term $a = 301$, common difference $d = 7$, and last term $l = 595$.
$$l = a + (n-1)d$$
$$595 = 301 + (n-1)(7)$$
$$294 = (n-1)(7)$$
$$n - 1 = 42$$
$$n = 43$$

**Step 4: Find the sum**

Using the sum formula $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(a + l)$:
$$S_{43} = \frac{43}{2}(301 + 595) = \frac{43}{2} \times 896 = 43 \times 448 = 19264$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{S_{43} = 19264}$$
