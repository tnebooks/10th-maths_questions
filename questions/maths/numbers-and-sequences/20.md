## The sequence $-3, -3, -3, \dots$ is

---

#### In the sequence $-3, -3, -3, \dots$:
1. The difference between consecutive terms is:
   $$d = -3 - (-3) = 0$$
   Since the common difference $d = 0$ is constant, the sequence is an **Arithmetic Progression (A.P.)**.
2. The ratio between consecutive terms is:
   $$r = \frac{-3}{-3} = 1$$
   Since the common ratio $r = 1$ is constant, the sequence is also a **Geometric Progression (G.P.)**.

Therefore, the sequence is **both an A.P. and a G.P.**
