## If $2 + 4 + 6 + \dots + 2k = 90$, then the value of $k$ is

---

#### The sum of the first $k$ even natural numbers is given by:
$$2 + 4 + 6 + \dots + 2k = 2(1 + 2 + 3 + \dots + k) = 2 \cdot \dfrac{k(k + 1)}{2} = k(k + 1)$$

Given that the sum equals $90$:
$$k(k + 1) = 90$$

Since $9 \times 10 = 90$, we have:
$$k = 9$$
