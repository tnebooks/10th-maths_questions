## $7^{4k} \equiv \ldots \pmod{100}$

---

#### First reduce the base power modulo $100$: $7^4 = 2401 = 24 \times 100 + 1$, so $7^4 \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$. Since congruences can be raised to any positive integer power (if $a \equiv b \pmod m$ then $a^k \equiv b^k \pmod m$), raising both sides to the power $k$ gives $7^{4k} = (7^4)^k \equiv 1^k \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$.
