## The next term of the sequence $\dfrac{3}{16}, \dfrac{1}{8}, \dfrac{1}{12}, \dfrac{1}{18}, \ldots$ is

---

#### Check whether the given sequence is a Geometric Progression by finding the ratio of consecutive terms.

$$\frac{t_2}{t_1} = \frac{1/8}{3/16} = \frac{1}{8} \times \frac{16}{3} = \frac{2}{3}$$

$$\frac{t_3}{t_2} = \frac{1/12}{1/8} = \frac{1}{12} \times \frac{8}{1} = \frac{8}{12} = \frac{2}{3}$$

$$\frac{t_4}{t_3} = \frac{1/18}{1/12} = \frac{1}{18} \times \frac{12}{1} = \frac{12}{18} = \frac{2}{3}$$

Since the ratio of consecutive terms is constant, the sequence is a Geometric Progression with common ratio $r = \dfrac{2}{3}$.

The next (5th) term:
$$t_5 = t_4 \times r = \frac{1}{18} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2}{54} = \frac{1}{27}$$

**Answer:** $\dfrac{1}{27}$
