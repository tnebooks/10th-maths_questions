## Find the GCD of $396$, $504$ and $636$ using Euclid's division algorithm.

---

#### Euclid's Division Algorithm: to find the GCD of two positive integers $a$ and $b$ (with $a > b$), repeatedly apply Euclid's Division Lemma $a = bq + r$, $0 \le r < b$, replacing $a$ by $b$ and $b$ by $r$, until the remainder becomes $0$. The divisor at that last step is the GCD.

**Step 1: Find GCD of $396$ and $504$**

Since $504 > 396$:
$$504 = 1 \times 396 + 108$$
$$396 = 3 \times 108 + 72$$
$$108 = 1 \times 72 + 36$$
$$72 = 2 \times 36 + 0$$

The remainder is now $0$, and the divisor at this stage is $36$.
$$\text{GCD}(396, 504) = 36$$

**Step 2: Find GCD of $36$ (result from Step 1) and $636$**

Since $636 > 36$:
$$636 = 17 \times 36 + 24$$
$$36 = 1 \times 24 + 12$$
$$24 = 2 \times 12 + 0$$

The remainder is now $0$, and the divisor at this stage is $12$.
$$\text{GCD}(36, 636) = 12$$

**Step 3: Conclusion**

$$\text{GCD}(396, 504, 636) = \text{GCD}(\text{GCD}(396, 504), 636) = \text{GCD}(36, 636) = 12$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\text{GCD}(396, 504, 636) = 12}$$
