## In a Geometric Progression, if $t_1 = \dfrac{1}{5}$ and $t_2 = \dfrac{1}{25}$, then the common ratio is

---

#### For a Geometric Progression, the common ratio $r$ is obtained by dividing any term by its preceding term: $r = \dfrac{t_2}{t_1}$.

$$r = \frac{\frac{1}{25}}{\frac{1}{5}} = \frac{1}{25} \times \frac{5}{1} = \frac{5}{25} = \frac{1}{5}$$
