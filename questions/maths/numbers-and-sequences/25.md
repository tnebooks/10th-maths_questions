## An A.P. consists of 31 terms. If its 16th term is $m$, then the sum of all the terms of this A.P. is

---

#### An A.P. with an odd number of terms has a unique middle term, and that middle term equals the average of all the terms (equivalently, the average of the first and last term).

Here the A.P. has $n = 31$ terms (odd), so the middle term is the $\dfrac{n+1}{2} = \dfrac{31+1}{2} = 16^{\text{th}}$ term.

So $t_{16} = m$ is the middle term, and for any A.P.:
$$\text{Middle term} = \frac{\text{Sum of all terms}}{\text{Number of terms}}$$

$$m = \frac{S_{31}}{31}$$

$$S_{31} = 31m$$

**Verification:** Take $a = 1, d = 1$ (A.P. $1,2,3,\ldots,31$). Then $t_{16} = 16 = m$. Sum $= \dfrac{31}{2}(1+31) = \dfrac{31}{2}(32) = 496$. Check: $31m = 31 \times 16 = 496$. ✓

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{31m}$$
