## $7^{4k} \equiv \text{___} \pmod{100}$

---

#### Use modular arithmetic: find $7^4 \pmod{100}$ first, then raise both sides to the power $k$.

$$7^2 = 49$$
$$7^4 = 7^2 \times 7^2 = 49 \times 49 = 2401$$

Reduce modulo $100$:
$$2401 = 24 \times 100 + 1 \implies 7^4 \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$$

Raising both sides to the power $k$ (congruences preserve powers):
$$7^{4k} = (7^4)^k \equiv 1^k \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$$

**Verification:** For $k=2$, $7^8 = (7^4)^2 = 2401^2 = 5{,}764{,}801$. Last two digits are $01$, confirming $7^8 \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$. ✓

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{1}$$
