## The value of $a_5$ in $a_n = (-1)^n \cdot n$ is

---

#### Substitute $n = 5$ directly into the given formula for the general term.

$$a_n = (-1)^n \cdot n$$

$$a_5 = (-1)^5 \cdot 5$$

Since $(-1)^5 = -1$ (an odd power of $-1$ is $-1$):

$$a_5 = -1 \times 5 = -5$$
