## The value of $(1^3+2^3+3^3+\cdots+15^3) - (1+2+3+\cdots+15)$ is

---

#### Use the special series formulas for the sum of the first $n$ natural numbers and the sum of the first $n$ cubes, with $n = 15$.

Sum of cubes: $1^3+2^3+\cdots+15^3 = \left[\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}\right]^2 = \left[\dfrac{15 \times 16}{2}\right]^2 = [120]^2 = 14400$.

Sum of natural numbers: $1+2+\cdots+15 = \dfrac{n(n+1)}{2} = \dfrac{15 \times 16}{2} = 120$.

Required value $= 14400 - 120 = 14280$.
