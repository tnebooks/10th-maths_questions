## The series $3+33+333+\cdots$ ($n$ terms) is

---

#### To classify the series, test whether consecutive terms have a constant difference (Arithmetic Progression) or a constant ratio (Geometric Progression).

**Step 1: Check for a common difference (A.P. test)**

Terms: $3, 33, 333, \ldots$
$$t_2 - t_1 = 33 - 3 = 30$$
$$t_3 - t_2 = 333 - 33 = 300$$

Since $30 \ne 300$, the difference is not constant — the series is **not an A.P.**

**Step 2: Check for a common ratio (G.P. test)**

$$\frac{t_2}{t_1} = \frac{33}{3} = 11$$
$$\frac{t_3}{t_2} = \frac{333}{33} = \frac{111}{11} \approx 10.09$$

Since $11 \ne \frac{333}{33}$, the ratio is not constant — the series is **not a G.P.** either.

**Conclusion:** The series is neither an Arithmetic Progression nor a Geometric Progression.

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\text{neither an A.P. nor a G.P.}}$$
