## The remainder obtained when the number $7 \times 13 \times 19 \times 23 \times 29 \times 31$ is divided by $6$ is

---

#### Instead of multiplying out the whole product, reduce each factor modulo $6$ first, then multiply the remainders using the modular arithmetic property $(a \times c) \equiv (b \times d) \pmod m$.

**Step 1: Reduce each factor mod $6$**
$$7 \equiv 1, \quad 13 \equiv 1, \quad 19 \equiv 1, \quad 23 \equiv 5, \quad 29 \equiv 5, \quad 31 \equiv 1 \pmod 6$$

**Step 2: Multiply the remainders mod $6$**
$$7 \times 13 \times 19 \times 23 \times 29 \times 31 \equiv 1 \times 1 \times 1 \times 5 \times 5 \times 1 \pmod 6$$
$$\equiv 25 \pmod 6$$
$$\equiv 1 \pmod 6 \quad (\because 25 = 4 \times 6 + 1)$$

**Answer:**
