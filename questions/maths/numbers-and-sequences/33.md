## If $a$ and $b$ are two positive integers where $a > 0$ and $b$ is a factor of $a$, then $\gcd(a, b)$ is

---

#### Since $b$ is a factor of $a$, $b$ divides $a$ completely without leaving a remainder. That is, $a = k \cdot b$ for some positive integer $k$.

The common factors of $a$ and $b$ are the divisors of $b$. The greatest among them is $b$ itself.

Therefore:
$$\gcd(a, b) = b$$
