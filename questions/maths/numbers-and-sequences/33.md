## Find the HCF of $396$, $504$, $636$.

---

#### Use Euclid's Division Algorithm repeatedly: first find the HCF of two of the numbers, then find the HCF of that result with the third number.

**Step 1: Find HCF of $396$ and $504$**

Apply $a = bq + r$ repeatedly, with $504 > 396$:
$$504 = 1 \times 396 + 108$$
$$396 = 3 \times 108 + 72$$
$$108 = 1 \times 72 + 36$$
$$72 = 2 \times 36 + 0$$

The last non-zero remainder is $36$, so $\text{HCF}(396, 504) = 36$.

**Step 2: Find HCF of $36$ and $636$**

$$636 = 17 \times 36 + 24$$
$$36 = 1 \times 24 + 12$$
$$24 = 2 \times 12 + 0$$

The last non-zero remainder is $12$, so $\text{HCF}(36, 636) = 12$.

**Step 3: Conclusion**

Since $\text{HCF}(396,504,636) = \text{HCF}\big(\text{HCF}(396,504),\,636\big) = \text{HCF}(36, 636) = 12$.

**Verification:** $396 = 12 \times 33$, $504 = 12 \times 42$, $636 = 12 \times 53$. All three quotients ($33, 42, 53$) share no common factor greater than $1$ (33=3×11, 42=2×3×7, 53 is prime), confirming $12$ is indeed the greatest common divisor. ✓

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{12}$$
