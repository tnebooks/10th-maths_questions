## $7^{4k} \equiv \underline{\phantom{xx}} \pmod{100}$

---

#### Compute the pattern of powers of $7$ modulo $100$ to find a cycle, then apply it to $7^{4k}$.

**Step 1: Compute successive powers of $7$ mod $100$**

$$7^1 = 7$$
$$7^2 = 49$$
$$7^3 = 343 \equiv 43 \pmod{100}$$
$$7^4 = 7^3 \times 7 = 343 \times 7 = 2401 \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$$

**Step 2: Use the cycle**

Since $7^4 \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$, raising both sides to the power $k$:
$$(7^4)^k \equiv 1^k \pmod{100}$$
$$7^{4k} \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$$

**Verification:** Check $k=2$: $7^8 = (7^4)^2 \equiv 1^2 = 1 \pmod{100}$. Directly, $7^4=2401$, $7^8=2401^2=5764801$, and $5764801 \mod 100 = 1$. ✓

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{1}$$
