## Find the sum of all natural numbers between $300$ and $500$ that are divisible by $11$.

---

#### The natural numbers between $300$ and $500$ divisible by $11$ form an Arithmetic Progression. Find the first term, last term, and number of terms, then apply the sum formula.

**Step 1: Find the first term**

The smallest multiple of $11$ greater than $300$: $300 \div 11 = 27.27\ldots$, so the next integer multiplier is $28$.
$$a = 11 \times 28 = 308$$

**Step 2: Find the last term**

The largest multiple of $11$ less than $500$: $500 \div 11 = 45.45\ldots$, so the largest multiplier is $45$.
$$l = 11 \times 45 = 495$$

**Step 3: Find the number of terms**

The multipliers run from $28$ to $45$ inclusive:
$$n = 45 - 28 + 1 = 18$$

**Step 4: Apply the sum formula**

$$S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a+l)$$

$$S_{18} = \frac{18}{2}(308+495) = 9 \times 803 = 7227$$

**Verification:** Common difference $d = 11$. Check $l = a+(n-1)d$: $308 + 17(11) = 308+187 = 495$. ✓ Direct check of a few terms: $308, 319, 330, \ldots, 484, 495$ — all divisible by $11$, confirmed by $308/11=28$ and $495/11=45$, both whole numbers. ✓

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{7227}$$
