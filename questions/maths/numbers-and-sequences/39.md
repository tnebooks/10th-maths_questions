## In a Geometric Progression, the $9^{\text{th}}$ term is $32805$ and the $6^{\text{th}}$ term is $1215$. Find the $12^{\text{th}}$ term.

---

#### Write $t_9$ and $t_6$ in terms of the first term $a$ and common ratio $r$, divide to eliminate $a$ and find $r^3$, then find $a$ and finally $t_{12}$.

**Step 1: Set up equations**

$$t_9 = ar^8 = 32805$$
$$t_6 = ar^5 = 1215$$

**Step 2: Find the common ratio**

Dividing:
$$\frac{ar^8}{ar^5} = \frac{32805}{1215}$$
$$r^3 = 27$$
$$r = 3$$

**Step 3: Find the first term**

From $ar^5 = 1215$:
$$a \times 3^5 = 1215$$
$$a \times 243 = 1215$$
$$a = \frac{1215}{243} = 5$$

**Step 4: Find the $12^{\text{th}}$ term**

$$t_{12} = ar^{11} = 5 \times 3^{11}$$

Since $3^{11} = 177147$:
$$t_{12} = 5 \times 177147 = 885735$$

**Verification:** $t_9 = ar^8 = 5 \times 3^8 = 5 \times 6561 = 32805$. ✓ $t_6 = ar^5 = 5 \times 243 = 1215$. ✓ Both match the given data, confirming $a=5$, $r=3$ are correct.

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{885735}$$
