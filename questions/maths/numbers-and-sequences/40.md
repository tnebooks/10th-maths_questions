## Rekha has 15 square colour papers of sizes $10\text{ cm}, 11\text{ cm}, 12\text{ cm}, \dots, 24\text{ cm}$. How much area can be decorated with these colour papers?

---

#### The total area that can be decorated by the 15 square colour papers is the sum of the areas of the individual squares:
$$\text{Total Area} = 10^2 + 11^2 + 12^2 + \dots + 24^2$$

This can be expressed as:
$$\sum_{k=10}^{24} k^2 = \sum_{k=1}^{24} k^2 - \sum_{k=1}^{9} k^2$$

Using the formula for the sum of squares of the first $n$ natural numbers, $\sum_{k=1}^n k^2 = \dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$:

1. For $n = 24$:
$$\sum_{k=1}^{24} k^2 = \frac{24 \times (24 + 1) \times (2(24) + 1)}{6} = \frac{24 \times 25 \times 49}{6} = 4 \times 25 \times 49 = 100 \times 49 = 4900$$

2. For $n = 9$:
$$\sum_{k=1}^{9} k^2 = \frac{9 \times (9 + 1) \times (2(9) + 1)}{6} = \frac{9 \times 10 \times 19}{6} = \frac{90 \times 19}{6} = 15 \times 19 = 285$$

Subtracting the two sums:
$$\text{Total Area} = 4900 - 285 = 4615\text{ cm}^2$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{4615\text{ cm}^2}$$
