## $7^{4k} \equiv$ ____ $(\text{mod } 100)$

---

#### Compute powers of 7 modulo 100: $7^2 = 49$, and $7^4 = (49)^2 = 2401 \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$. Therefore, $7^{4k} = (7^4)^k \equiv 1^k \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$.
