## The range of the relation $R = \{(x, x^2) \mid x \text{ is a prime number less than } 13\}$ is

---

#### The prime numbers less than 13 are $2, 3, 5, 7, 11$. These form the domain of the relation. The range consists of the images $x^2$ for each $x$ in the domain:
$$2^2 = 4, \quad 3^2 = 9, \quad 5^2 = 25, \quad 7^2 = 49, \quad 11^2 = 121$$
Therefore, the range is $\{4, 9, 25, 49, 121\}$.
