## A function $f: A \to B$ is defined by $f(x) = \frac{x}{2} - 1$, where $A = \{2, 4, 6, 10, 12\}$ and $B = \{0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 9\}$. Represent $f$ by: (i) a set of ordered pairs (ii) a table (iii) an arrow diagram (iv) a graph

---

#### Given $A = \{2, 4, 6, 10, 12\}$, $B = \{0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 9\}$, and $f(x) = \frac{x}{2} - 1$.

Evaluating the image of each element of $A$ under $f$:
$$f(2) = \frac{2}{2} - 1 = 1 - 1 = 0$$
$$f(4) = \frac{4}{2} - 1 = 2 - 1 = 1$$
$$f(6) = \frac{6}{2} - 1 = 3 - 1 = 2$$
$$f(10) = \frac{10}{2} - 1 = 5 - 1 = 4$$
$$f(12) = \frac{12}{2} - 1 = 6 - 1 = 5$$

**(i) Set of ordered pairs:**
$$f = \{(2, 0), (4, 1), (6, 2), (10, 4), (12, 5)\}$$

**(ii) Table:**

| $x$ | 2 | 4 | 6 | 10 | 12 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $f(x)$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 4 | 5 |

**(iii) Arrow diagram:**
Each element of $A$ is connected by an arrow to its corresponding image in $B$:
- $2 \to 0$
- $4 \to 1$
- $6 \to 2$
- $10 \to 4$
- $12 \to 5$

(Note: $9 \in B$ has no pre-image in $A$, so no arrow points to it.)

**(iv) Graph:**
Plot the ordered pairs on the Cartesian plane:
$$(2, 0),\ (4, 1),\ (6, 2),\ (10, 4),\ (12, 5)$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{f = \{(2, 0), (4, 1), (6, 2), (10, 4), (12, 5)\}}$$
