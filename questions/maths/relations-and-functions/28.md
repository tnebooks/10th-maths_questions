## $A = \{1,2,3,4\}$ and $B = \{2,5,8,11,14\}$ are two sets. A function $f : A \to B$ is given by $f(x) = 3x - 1$. Represent this function using (i) an arrow diagram (ii) a table (iii) a set of ordered pairs (iv) a graph.

---

#### Given $A = \{1,2,3,4\}$, $B = \{2,5,8,11,14\}$, and $f(x) = 3x - 1$.

Evaluating the image of each element of $A$ under $f$:
$$f(1) = 3(1) - 1 = 3 - 1 = 2$$
$$f(2) = 3(2) - 1 = 6 - 1 = 5$$
$$f(3) = 3(3) - 1 = 9 - 1 = 8$$
$$f(4) = 3(4) - 1 = 12 - 1 = 11$$

**(i) Arrow diagram:**
Each element of $A$ is connected by an arrow to its corresponding image in $B$:
- $1 \to 2$
- $2 \to 5$
- $3 \to 8$
- $4 \to 11$

(Note: $14 \in B$ has no pre-image in $A$, so no arrow points to it.)

**(ii) Table:**

| $x$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $f(x)$ | 2 | 5 | 8 | 11 |

**(iii) Set of ordered pairs:**
$$f = \{(1, 2), (2, 5), (3, 8), (4, 11)\}$$

**(iv) Graph:**
Plot the ordered pairs on the Cartesian plane:
$$(1, 2),\ (2, 5),\ (3, 8),\ (4, 11)$$
These points lie on a straight line, since $f(x) = 3x - 1$ is a linear function.

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{f = \{(1, 2), (2, 5), (3, 8), (4, 11)\}}$$
