## If $f(x) = 2x+3$, $g(x) = 1-2x$ and $h(x) = 3x$, then prove that $f \circ (g \circ h) = (f \circ g) \circ h$.

---

#### Given $f(x) = 2x + 3$, $g(x) = 1 - 2x$, $h(x) = 3x$. We must show that composition of functions is associative for these three functions, i.e., $f \circ (g \circ h) = (f \circ g) \circ h$.

**Step 1: Compute $g \circ h$.**
$$(g \circ h)(x) = g(h(x)) = g(3x) = 1 - 2(3x) = 1 - 6x$$

**Step 2: Compute $f \circ (g \circ h)$.**
$$f \circ (g \circ h)(x) = f(1 - 6x) = 2(1 - 6x) + 3 = 2 - 12x + 3 = 5 - 12x$$

**Step 3: Compute $f \circ g$.**
$$(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x)) = f(1 - 2x) = 2(1 - 2x) + 3 = 2 - 4x + 3 = 5 - 4x$$

**Step 4: Compute $(f \circ g) \circ h$.**
$$(f \circ g) \circ h(x) = (f \circ g)(3x) = 5 - 4(3x) = 5 - 12x$$

**Step 5: Compare.**
From Step 2: $f \circ (g \circ h)(x) = 5 - 12x$.
From Step 4: $(f \circ g) \circ h(x) = 5 - 12x$.

Since both results are identical, we have proved:
$$\mathbf{f \circ (g \circ h) = (f \circ g) \circ h = 5 - 12x}$$
