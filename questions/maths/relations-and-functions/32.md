## Let $n(A) = m$ and $n(B) = n$. Then the total number of relations from $A$ to $B$ is

---

#### A relation from $A$ to $B$ is any subset of $A \times B$. Since $n(A) = m$ and $n(B) = n$, the number of elements in $A \times B$ is $n(A \times B) = n(A) \times n(B) = mn$. The number of subsets of a set with $mn$ elements is $2^{mn}$. Therefore, the total number of relations from $A$ to $B$ is $2^{mn}$.
