## If $f(x) = x^2$ and $g(x) = x - 2$, then $f \circ g$ is

---

#### By definition of composite functions:
$$(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$$
Given $g(x) = x - 2$ and $f(x) = x^2$:
$$f(g(x)) = f(x - 2) = (x - 2)^2$$
Hence, $f \circ g = (x - 2)^2$.
