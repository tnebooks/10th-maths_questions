## If $f(x) = x - 1$, $g(x) = 3x + 1$ and $h(x) = x^2$, then prove that $(f \circ g) \circ h = f \circ (g \circ h)$.

---

#### Given $f(x) = x - 1$, $g(x) = 3x + 1$, $h(x) = x^2$. We must show that $(f \circ g) \circ h = f \circ (g \circ h)$.

**Step 1: Compute $f \circ g$.**
$$(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x)) = f(3x+1) = (3x+1) - 1 = 3x$$

**Step 2: Compute $(f \circ g) \circ h$.**
$$(f \circ g) \circ h(x) = (f \circ g)(h(x)) = (f \circ g)(x^2) = 3x^2$$

**Step 3: Compute $g \circ h$.**
$$(g \circ h)(x) = g(h(x)) = g(x^2) = 3x^2 + 1$$

**Step 4: Compute $f \circ (g \circ h)$.**
$$f \circ (g \circ h)(x) = f(3x^2 + 1) = (3x^2 + 1) - 1 = 3x^2$$

**Step 5: Compare.**
From Step 2: $(f \circ g) \circ h(x) = 3x^2$.
From Step 4: $f \circ (g \circ h)(x) = 3x^2$.

Since both results are identical, we have proved:
$$\mathbf{(f \circ g) \circ h = f \circ (g \circ h) = 3x^2}$$
