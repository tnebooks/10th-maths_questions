## Let $A$ = the set of all natural numbers less than $8$, $B$ = the set of all prime numbers less than $8$, $C$ = the set of even prime numbers. Verify that $(A \cap B) \times C = (A \times C) \cap (B \times C)$.

---

#### Given: $A$ = natural numbers less than $8$, $B$ = prime numbers less than $8$, $C$ = even prime numbers.

**Step 1: Write out the sets.**
$$A = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\}$$
$$B = \{2, 3, 5, 7\}$$

The only even prime number is $2$, so:
$$C = \{2\}$$

**Step 2: Find $A \cap B$.**
Since every element of $B$ is also in $A$ (that is, $B \subseteq A$):
$$A \cap B = \{2, 3, 5, 7\}$$

**Step 3: Find $(A \cap B) \times C$.**
$$(A \cap B) \times C = \{2, 3, 5, 7\} \times \{2\} = \{(2,2), (3,2), (5,2), (7,2)\}$$

**Step 4: Find $A \times C$.**
$$A \times C = \{1,2,3,4,5,6,7\} \times \{2\} = \{(1,2), (2,2), (3,2), (4,2), (5,2), (6,2), (7,2)\}$$

**Step 5: Find $B \times C$.**
$$B \times C = \{2,3,5,7\} \times \{2\} = \{(2,2), (3,2), (5,2), (7,2)\}$$

**Step 6: Find $(A \times C) \cap (B \times C)$.**
$$(A \times C) \cap (B \times C) = \{(1,2),(2,2),(3,2),(4,2),(5,2),(6,2),(7,2)\} \cap \{(2,2),(3,2),(5,2),(7,2)\}$$
$$= \{(2,2), (3,2), (5,2), (7,2)\}$$

**Step 7: Compare.**
From Step 3: $(A \cap B) \times C = \{(2,2), (3,2), (5,2), (7,2)\}$.
From Step 6: $(A \times C) \cap (B \times C) = \{(2,2), (3,2), (5,2), (7,2)\}$.

Since both sides are equal, we have verified:
$$\mathbf{(A \cap B) \times C = (A \times C) \cap (B \times C) = \{(2,2), (3,2), (5,2), (7,2)\}}$$
