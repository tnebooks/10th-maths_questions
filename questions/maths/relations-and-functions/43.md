## If $A = \{1, 2, 3\}$, $B = \{2, 3, 5\}$, $C = \{3, 4\}$ and $D = \{1, 3, 5\}$, verify that $(A \cap C) \times (B \cap D) = (A \times B) \cap (C \times D)$.

---

#### Given: $A = \{1, 2, 3\}$, $B = \{2, 3, 5\}$, $C = \{3, 4\}$, $D = \{1, 3, 5\}$.

**Step 1: Compute the Left Hand Side (LHS).**

$$A \cap C = \{1, 2, 3\} \cap \{3, 4\} = \{3\}$$
$$B \cap D = \{2, 3, 5\} \cap \{1, 3, 5\} = \{3, 5\}$$

$$\text{LHS} = (A \cap C) \times (B \cap D) = \{3\} \times \{3, 5\} = \{(3,3), (3,5)\} \quad \text{...(1)}$$

**Step 2: Compute the Right Hand Side (RHS).**

$$A \times B = \{1,2,3\} \times \{2,3,5\} = \{(1,2),(1,3),(1,5),(2,2),(2,3),(2,5),(3,2),(3,3),(3,5)\}$$

$$C \times D = \{3,4\} \times \{1,3,5\} = \{(3,1),(3,3),(3,5),(4,1),(4,3),(4,5)\}$$

Taking the common ordered pairs of $A \times B$ and $C \times D$:
$$\text{RHS} = (A \times B) \cap (C \times D) = \{(3,3), (3,5)\} \quad \text{...(2)}$$

**Step 3: Compare (1) and (2).**
$$\text{LHS} = \{(3,3),(3,5)\} = \text{RHS}$$

Hence, **$(A \cap C) \times (B \cap D) = (A \times B) \cap (C \times D)$ is verified.**
