## If $A$ and $B$ are two finite sets such that $n(A) = m$ and $n(B) = n$, then the total number of functions that can be defined from $A$ to $B$ is

---

#### For each of the $m$ elements in the domain $A$, there are $n(B) = n$ possible choices for its image in the codomain $B$. By the fundamental principle of counting, the total number of functions from $A$ to $B$ is:
$$\underbrace{n \times n \times \dots \times n}_{m\text{ times}} = n^m$$
