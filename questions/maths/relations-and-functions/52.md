## A function $f: A \to B$ is defined by $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{2} - 1$, where $A = \{2, 4, 6, 10, 12\}$ and $B = \{0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 9\}$. Represent $f$ by:
(i) set of ordered pairs
(ii) an arrow diagram
(iii) a table
(iv) a graph

---

#### Given $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{2} - 1$:
- For $x = 2$: $f(2) = \dfrac{2}{2} - 1 = 1 - 1 = 0$
- For $x = 4$: $f(4) = \dfrac{4}{2} - 1 = 2 - 1 = 1$
- For $x = 6$: $f(6) = \dfrac{6}{2} - 1 = 3 - 1 = 2$
- For $x = 10$: $f(10) = \dfrac{10}{2} - 1 = 5 - 1 = 4$
- For $x = 12$: $f(12) = \dfrac{12}{2} - 1 = 6 - 1 = 5$

**(i) Set of ordered pairs:**
$$f = \{(2, 0), (4, 1), (6, 2), (10, 4), (12, 5)\}$$

**(ii) Arrow diagram:**
Each element $x \in A$ is mapped to its image $f(x) \in B$:
- $2 \to 0$
- $4 \to 1$
- $6 \to 2$
- $10 \to 4$
- $12 \to 5$
*(Note that $9 \in B$ does not have a pre-image in $A$.)*

**(iii) A table:**

| $x$ | 2 | 4 | 6 | 10 | 12 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $f(x)$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 4 | 5 |

**(iv) A graph:**
In the $xy$-plane, plot the points $(2, 0), (4, 1), (6, 2), (10, 4), (12, 5)$.
