## If $f(x) = x - 4$, $g(x) = x^2$ and $h(x) = 3x - 5$, show that $(f \circ g) \circ h = f \circ (g \circ h)$.

---

#### **Given functions:**
$$f(x) = x - 4, \quad g(x) = x^2, \quad h(x) = 3x - 5$$

**Step 1: Compute LHS = $(f \circ g) \circ h$**
First find $f \circ g(x)$:
$$f \circ g(x) = f(g(x)) = f(x^2) = x^2 - 4$$

Now compose with $h(x)$:
$$(f \circ g) \circ h(x) = (f \circ g)(h(x))$$
$$= (f \circ g)(3x - 5)$$
$$= (3x - 5)^2 - 4$$
$$= (9x^2 - 30x + 25) - 4$$
$$= 9x^2 - 30x + 21 \quad \text{--- (1)}$$

**Step 2: Compute RHS = $f \circ (g \circ h)$**
First find $g \circ h(x)$:
$$g \circ h(x) = g(h(x)) = g(3x - 5) = (3x - 5)^2 = 9x^2 - 30x + 25$$

Now compose with $f(x)$:
$$f \circ (g \circ h)(x) = f(g \circ h(x))$$
$$= f(9x^2 - 30x + 25)$$
$$= (9x^2 - 30x + 25) - 4$$
$$= 9x^2 - 30x + 21 \quad \text{--- (2)}$$

**Step 3: Compare LHS and RHS**
From (1) and (2):
$$(f \circ g) \circ h(x) = f \circ (g \circ h)(x) = 9x^2 - 30x + 21$$

Hence, $(f \circ g) \circ h = f \circ (g \circ h)$ is shown (composition of functions is associative).
