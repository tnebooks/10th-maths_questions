## The mean of the first $n$ natural numbers is

---

#### The first $n$ natural numbers are $1, 2, 3, \dots, n$.

The sum of the first $n$ natural numbers is given by:
$$\sum_{i=1}^n i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

The arithmetic mean (average) $\bar{x}$ is defined as the sum of observations divided by the total number of observations ($n$):
$$\bar{x} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n i}{n} = \frac{\frac{n(n+1)}{2}}{n} = \frac{n+1}{2}$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\dfrac{n+1}{2}}$$
