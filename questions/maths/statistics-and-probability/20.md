## Two dice are rolled. Find the probability that the sum of outcomes is:
(i) equal to $4$
(ii) greater than $10$
(iii) less than $13$

---

#### The sample space $S$ has $n(S) = 36$ outcomes.

**(i) Event $A$: Sum of outcomes is equal to 4**
$$A = \{(1,3), (2,2), (3,1)\}$$
$$n(A) = 3 \implies P(A) = \frac{n(A)}{n(S)} = \frac{3}{36} = \frac{1}{12}$$

**(ii) Event $B$: Sum of outcomes is greater than 10**
The sum can be 11 or 12:
$$B = \{(5,6), (6,5), (6,6)\}$$
$$n(B) = 3 \implies P(B) = \frac{n(B)}{n(S)} = \frac{3}{36} = \frac{1}{12}$$

**(iii) Event $C$: Sum of outcomes is less than 13**
The maximum possible sum is $6 + 6 = 12$, so every outcome in $S$ has a sum less than 13.
$$C = S \implies n(C) = n(S) = 36$$
$$P(C) = \frac{36}{36} = 1$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\text{(i) } \frac{1}{12}, \quad \text{(ii) } \frac{1}{12}, \quad \text{(iii) } 1}$$
