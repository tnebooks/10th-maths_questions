## In the experiment of tossing a coin three times, the probability of getting 3 heads or 3 tails is

---

#### When a coin is tossed three times, the sample space is:
$$S = \{HHH, HHT, HTH, HTT, THH, THT, TTH, TTT\}$$
$$n(S) = 8$$

Let $E$ be the event of getting 3 heads or 3 tails:
$$E = \{HHH, TTT\}$$
$$n(E) = 2$$

Therefore, the probability is:
$$P(E) = \dfrac{n(E)}{n(S)} = \dfrac{2}{8} = \dfrac{1}{4}$$
