## A bag contains $5$ red balls, $6$ white balls, $7$ green balls and $8$ black balls. One ball is drawn at random from the bag. Find the probability that the ball drawn is:
(i) white
(ii) black or red
(iii) not white
(iv) neither white nor black

---

#### Total number of balls in the bag:
$$n(S) = 5 + 6 + 7 + 8 = 26$$

**(i) Probability that the ball drawn is white:**
Number of white balls $n(W) = 6$.
$$P(W) = \frac{n(W)}{n(S)} = \frac{6}{26} = \frac{3}{13}$$

**(ii) Probability that the ball drawn is black or red:**
Number of black or red balls $n(B \cup R) = 8 + 5 = 13$.
$$P(B \cup R) = \frac{13}{26} = \frac{1}{2}$$

**(iii) Probability that the ball drawn is not white:**
Number of non-white balls $= 26 - 6 = 20$.
$$P(W') = 1 - P(W) = 1 - \frac{3}{13} = \frac{10}{13}$$

**(iv) Probability that the ball drawn is neither white nor black:**
Balls that are neither white nor black are red and green balls:
Number of red and green balls $= 5 + 7 = 12$.
$$P(\text{neither}) = \frac{12}{26} = \frac{6}{13}$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\text{(i) } \frac{3}{13}, \quad \text{(ii) } \frac{1}{2}, \quad \text{(iii) } \frac{10}{13}, \quad \text{(iv) } \frac{6}{13}}$$
