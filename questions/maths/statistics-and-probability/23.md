## The marks scored by students in a class test are given below. Calculate the standard deviation of their marks:

| Marks ($x$) | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Frequency ($f$) | 7 | 3 | 5 | 9 | 5 |

---

#### Let assumed mean $A = 8$.

We prepare the following calculation table:

| $x$ | $f$ | $d = x - A = x - 8$ | $fd$ | $fd^2$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 4 | 7 | $-4$ | $-28$ | 112 |
| 6 | 3 | $-2$ | $-6$ | 12 |
| 8 | 5 | $0$ | $0$ | 0 |
| 10 | 9 | $2$ | 18 | 36 |
| 12 | 5 | $4$ | 20 | 80 |
| **Total** | $N = \sum f = 29$ | | $\sum fd = 4$ | $\sum fd^2 = 240$ |

**Standard Deviation Formula (Assumed Mean Method):**
$$\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum fd^2}{N} - \left(\frac{\sum fd}{N}\right)^2}$$

Substituting the values:
$$\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{240}{29} - \left(\frac{4}{29}\right)^2} = \sqrt{8.2759 - 0.0190} = \sqrt{8.2569} \approx 2.87$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{\sigma \approx 2.87}$$
