## The instrument used to measure the angle of elevation and the angle of depression is

---

#### A **theodolite** is a precision optical instrument for measuring angles between designated visible points in the horizontal and vertical planes, primarily used in surveying, navigation, and indirect measurement of heights and distances (angles of elevation and depression).
