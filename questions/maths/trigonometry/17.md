## The value of $\tan\theta\csc^2\theta - \tan\theta$ is

---

#### Factor $\tan\theta$ out of the expression:
$$\tan\theta\csc^2\theta - \tan\theta = \tan\theta(\csc^2\theta - 1)$$

Using the Pythagorean identity $\csc^2\theta - 1 = \cot^2\theta$:
$$\tan\theta(\csc^2\theta - 1) = \tan\theta\cot^2\theta$$

Writing $\tan\theta = \dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ and $\cot\theta = \dfrac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$, so $\cot^2\theta = \dfrac{\cos^2\theta}{\sin^2\theta}$:
$$\tan\theta\cot^2\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} \times \frac{\cos^2\theta}{\sin^2\theta} = \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta} = \cot\theta$$

**Answer:** $\cot\theta$
