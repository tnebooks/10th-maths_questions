## From the top of a $60\text{ m}$ high lighthouse, the angles of depression of two ships are observed as $28^\circ$ and $45^\circ$. If one ship is directly behind the other on the same side of the lighthouse, find the distance between the two ships. ($\tan 28^\circ = 0.5317$)

---

#### Let $AB$ represent the lighthouse of height $h = 60\text{ m}$.
Let $C$ and $D$ be the two ships on the sea level, with $D$ being further away from the lighthouse along the straight line $BCD$.

The angles of depression of ships $C$ and $D$ are $45^\circ$ and $28^\circ$ respectively. By alternate interior angles:
$$\angle ACB = 45^\circ \quad \text{and} \quad \angle ADB = 28^\circ$$

1. In right-angled triangle $\triangle ABC$:
   $$\tan 45^\circ = \frac{AB}{BC}$$
   $$1 = \frac{60}{BC} \implies BC = 60\text{ m}$$

2. In right-angled triangle $\triangle ABD$:
   $$\tan 28^\circ = \frac{AB}{BD}$$
   $$0.5317 = \frac{60}{BD} \implies BD = \frac{60}{0.5317} = 112.85\text{ m}$$

The distance between the two ships is $CD$:
$$CD = BD - BC = 112.85 - 60 = 52.85\text{ m}$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{52.85\text{ m}}$$
