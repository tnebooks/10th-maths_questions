## A $1.2\text{ m}$ tall girl spots a balloon moving with the wind in a horizontal line at a height of $88.2\text{ m}$ from the ground. The angle of elevation of the balloon from the eyes of the girl at an instant is $60^\circ$. After some time, the angle of elevation reduces to $30^\circ$. Find the distance travelled by the balloon during the interval.

---

#### Let the initial position of the balloon be $P$ and its final position be $Q$.

- Height of the balloon from ground = $88.2\text{ m}$
- Height of the girl = $1.2\text{ m}$
- Height of the balloon above the eye level of the girl:
  $$h = 88.2 - 1.2 = 87\text{ m}$$

Let the horizontal line at eye level be the line along which horizontal distances are measured. Let $A$ be the eye of the girl.
- Initial horizontal distance from the girl to the balloon = $x_1$
- Final horizontal distance from the girl to the balloon = $x_2$

**Step 1: In right-angled triangle formed at initial position (angle $60^\circ$)**
$$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{h}{x_1}$$
$$\sqrt{3} = \frac{87}{x_1} \implies x_1 = \frac{87}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{87\sqrt{3}}{3} = 29\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$

**Step 2: In right-angled triangle formed at final position (angle $30^\circ$)**
$$\tan 30^\circ = \frac{h}{x_2}$$
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{87}{x_2} \implies x_2 = 87\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$

**Step 3: Distance travelled by the balloon**
$$\text{Distance} = x_2 - x_1 = 87\sqrt{3} - 29\sqrt{3} = (87 - 29)\sqrt{3} = 58\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$

Taking $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$:
$$\text{Distance} = 58 \times 1.732 \approx 100.46\text{ m}$$

**Answer:**
$$\mathbf{58\sqrt{3}\text{ m}}$$
